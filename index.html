Our Story

A roastery that started with one drum and a borrowed kitchen.

Ember began as a Sunday hobby — a 1kg drum roaster on a balcony, and a notebook full of burnt batches. A decade later, the notebook is full of something better: ratios, roast curves, and the names of the farms we now visit every season.

We still roast in small batches, twice a week, and we still taste every single one before it goes on the shelf.

How it gets to you

From farm to cup, in four steps.

Every bag traces back to a farm we've visited and a roast curve we've tasted.

01 — Source

Green Bean

We buy direct from smallholder farms, often a season ahead, so growers can plan around a guaranteed price.

02 — Roast

Small Batch

Twice-weekly roasts in 5kg drums, each tracked by curve and cupped before approval.

03 — Grind

To Order

Beans are ground per cup, dialled to the specific brew method — no pre-ground stock, ever.

04 — Brew

Hand Poured

Filter, espresso, or cold drip — each method timed and weighed by the person making it.
